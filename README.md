
# ACI Dealing Certificate New Version Exam Guide

[![ACI FMA Certification](https://img.shields.io/badge/ACI%20FMA-Dealing%20Certificate-003B5C?style=for-the-badge)](https://acifma.com/aci-dealing-certificate) [![Certificate Type](https://img.shields.io/badge/Certificate-Financial%20Markets-1A3B5C?style=for-the-badge)](https://acifma.com/aci-dealing-certificate)

A dedicated study guide for candidates preparing for the **ACI Dealing Certificate New Version Exam (002-101)**, covering financial markets, foreign exchange, interest rates, derivatives, and market applications.

---

## Exam Overview

The ACI Dealing Certificate New Version validates foundational knowledge of financial markets and the instruments used in foreign exchange, money and interest rate markets, and fixed-income, currency, and commodities derivatives.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based multiple-choice exam delivered in English at selected test centres.
- **Target Audience**: New entrants and junior dealers, middle-office and operations personnel, compliance officers, and risk officers.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal prerequisite is stated on the official exam page; the syllabus is designed for candidates entering financial markets roles.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Financial Markets Environment

- Financial market structure, participants, market segments, and transaction life cycles.
- Regulated and over-the-counter markets, market-making, and financial market instruments.
- FX Global Code, Global Precious Metals Code, UK Money Markets Code, and relevant regulations.

### 2. Foreign Exchange

- Currency quotations, bid and offer rates, currency pairs, pips, and exchange-rate calculations.
- FX spot, outright forwards, swaps, forward points, and non-deliverable forwards.
- Precious metals markets, quotations, benchmark fixings, and associated risks.

### 3. Rates (Money and Interest Rate Markets)

- Money market instruments, interest calculations, discounting, compounding, and value dates.
- Interest-rate benchmarks, yield curves, bonds, and forward-forward transactions.
- Repurchase agreements, collateral, haircuts, and securities financing transactions.

### 4. FICC Derivatives

- Derivative instruments, market terminology, applications, benefits, and risks.
- Currency and interest-rate options, calls, puts, premiums, and strike prices.
- Derivative valuation concepts, payoff profiles, and calculations covered by the syllabus.

### 5. Financial Markets Applications

- Practical application of financial market instruments and dealing concepts.
- Market conventions, transaction calculations, and instrument-related risks.
- Application of financial markets knowledge to common professional scenarios.
